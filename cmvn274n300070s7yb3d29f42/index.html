力学-3.1 哈密顿力学——辛形式与正则方程

上一篇，我们从对称性出发得到了诺特定理：作用量的每个连续对称性，都对应一条守恒律。空间平移给出动量守恒，时间平移给出能量守恒；在场论中，时空平移又给出了正则能动张量，它的各个分量分别描述能量密度、动量密度、能流密度和动量流密度。不过，这些讨论都建立在拉格朗日框架之下：基本变量是坐标 qqq 与速度 q˙\dot{q}q˙​，运动方程是二阶微分方程。这并不是描述力学系统的唯一方式。接下来，我们将换一组变量来描述系统，并看看动力学由此显露出怎样的几何结构。

哈密顿力学的内容较多，分为力学-3.1、3.2、3.3 三篇。本篇建立相空间与辛形式，导出正则方程，并讨论哈密顿向量场与泊松括号；力学-3.2 讨论正则变换与哈密顿—雅可比方程；力学-3.3 讨论可积系统，以及哈密顿由这套理论发现的光学与力学之间的类比。

LXHaω=d(ωabXHb)+(dω)abcXc=d(ωabXHb)=d(dH)a=0□\begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}_{X^a_\mathscr{H}}\omega&=\mathrm{d}(\omega_{ab}X^b_\mathscr{H})+(\mathrm{d}\omega)_{abc}X^c\\ &=\mathrm{d}(\omega_{ab}X^b_\mathscr{H})\\ &=\mathrm{d}(\mathrm{d}\mathscr{H})_a\\ &=0 \end{aligned}\tag*{$\square$} LXHa​​ω​=d(ωab​XHb​)+(dω)abc​Xc=d(ωab​XHb​)=d(dH)a​=0​□

由此立即得到，相空间的体积形式同样沿哈密顿向量场保持不变，这就是刘维尔定理：

LXHaΩ=0\mathcal{L}_{X^a_\mathscr{H}}\Omega=0 LXHa​​Ω=0

按定义展开，并利用保辛性

LXHaΩ=LXHa(1n!ω∧n)=1n!LXHa(ω∧n)=1n!∑k=1nω∧(k−1)∧(LXHaω)∧ω∧(n−k)=0□\begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}_{X^a_\mathscr{H}}\Omega&=\mathcal{L}_{X^a_\mathscr{H}}(\frac{1}{n!}\omega^{\wedge n})\\ &=\frac{1}{n!}\mathcal{L}_{X^a_\mathscr{H}}(\omega^{\wedge n})\\ &=\frac{1}{n!}\sum_{k=1}^n\omega^{\wedge(k-1)}\wedge(\mathcal{L}_{X^a_\mathscr{H}}\omega)\wedge\omega^{\wedge(n-k)}\\ &=0 \end{aligned}\tag*{$\square$} LXHa​​Ω​=LXHa​​(n!1​ω∧n)=n!1​LXHa​​(ω∧n)=n!1​k=1∑n​ω∧(k−1)∧(LXHa​​ω)∧ω∧(n−k)=0​□

刘维尔定理可以这样理解：在相空间中取一团代表点，随着时间演化，这团点可以被拉伸、扭曲，形状变得面目全非，但它占据的体积始终不变，就像不可压缩的液体在流动中改变形状而体积不变。用 ρ(q,p,t)\rho(q,p,t)ρ(q,p,t) 表示代表点的密度。体积不变，再加上代表点的数目守恒（点既不产生也不消失），就给出密度沿轨道守恒，也就是

dρdt=∂ρ∂qμq˙μ+∂ρ∂pμp˙μ+∂ρ∂t=0\frac{\mathrm{d}\rho}{\mathrm{d}t}=\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial q^\mu}\dot{q}^\mu+\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial p_\mu}\dot{p}_\mu+\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}=0 dtdρ​=∂qμ∂ρ​q˙​μ+∂pμ​∂ρ​p˙​μ​+∂t∂ρ​=0

用正则方程代换，得到

dρdt=∂ρ∂qμ∂H∂pμ−∂ρ∂pμ∂H∂qμ+∂ρ∂t=0\frac{\mathrm{d}\rho}{\mathrm{d}t}=\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial q^\mu}\frac{\partial\mathscr{H}}{\partial p_\mu}-\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial p_\mu}\frac{\partial\mathscr{H}}{\partial q^\mu}+\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}=0 dtdρ​=∂qμ∂ρ​∂pμ​∂H​−∂pμ​∂ρ​∂qμ∂H​+∂t∂ρ​=0

用下一节介绍的泊松括号，上式可以写成

dρdt={ρ,H}+∂ρ∂t=0\frac{\mathrm{d}\rho}{\mathrm{d}t}=\{\rho,\mathscr{H}\}+\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}=0 dtdρ​={ρ,H}+∂t∂ρ​=0

泊松括号

哈密顿量通过辛形式确定了哈密顿向量场。对相空间上的任意标量场 FFF，同样可以这样确定一个向量场

XFa=ωab(dF)bX^a_F=\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F)_b XFa​=ωab(dF)b​

我们定义两个光滑标量场 FFF 和 GGG 的泊松括号

{F,G}:=XG(F)=ωab(dF)a(dG)b=ωabXGaXFb=∂F∂qμ∂G∂pμ−∂F∂pμ∂G∂qμ\{F,G\}:=X_G(F)=\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F)_a(\mathrm{d}G)_b=\omega_{ab}X^a_GX^b_F=\frac{\partial F}{\partial q^\mu}\frac{\partial G}{\partial p_\mu}-\frac{\partial F}{\partial p_\mu}\frac{\partial G}{\partial q^\mu} {F,G}:=XG​(F)=ωab(dF)a​(dG)b​=ωab​XGa​XFb​=∂qμ∂F​∂pμ​∂G​−∂pμ​∂F​∂qμ∂G​

沿真实运动，标量场对时间的导数为

F˙=∂F∂qμq˙μ+∂F∂pμp˙μ+∂F∂t\dot{F}=\frac{\partial F}{\partial q^\mu}\dot{q}^\mu+\frac{\partial F}{\partial p_\mu}\dot{p}_\mu+\frac{\partial F}{\partial t} F˙=∂qμ∂F​q˙​μ+∂pμ​∂F​p˙​μ​+∂t∂F​

代入正则方程

F˙=∂F∂qμ∂H∂pμ−∂F∂pμ∂H∂qμ+∂F∂t={F,H}+∂F∂t\begin{gathered} \dot{F}=\frac{\partial F}{\partial q^\mu}\frac{\partial\mathscr{H}}{\partial p_\mu}-\frac{\partial F}{\partial p_\mu}\frac{\partial\mathscr{H}}{\partial q^\mu}+\frac{\partial F}{\partial t}\\ =\{F,\mathscr{H}\}+\frac{\partial F}{\partial t} \end{gathered} F˙=∂qμ∂F​∂pμ​∂H​−∂pμ​∂F​∂qμ∂H​+∂t∂F​={F,H}+∂t∂F​​

如果 FFF 不显含时间，它的变化率就是它与哈密顿量的泊松括号。特别地，正则方程可以写成

q˙μ={qμ,H}p˙μ={pμ,H}\begin{gathered} \dot{q}^\mu=\{q^\mu,\mathscr{H}\}\\ \dot{p}_\mu=\{p_\mu,\mathscr{H}\} \end{gathered} q˙​μ={qμ,H}p˙​μ​={pμ​,H}​

泊松括号不只是计算工具，它还为相空间上的标量场提供了一种代数结构。它满足以下性质，下面逐条证明，不关心证明的读者可以直接跳到下一节。

  • {F,G}=−{G,F}\{F,G\}=-\{G,F\}{F,G}=−{G,F}

{F,G}=ωab(dF)a(dG)b=ωba(dF)b(dG)a=−ωab(dG)a(dF)b=−{G,F}□\begin{aligned} \{F,G\}&=\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F)_a(\mathrm{d}G)_b\\ &=\omega^{ba}(\mathrm{d}F)_b(\mathrm{d}G)_a\\ &=-\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}G)_a(\mathrm{d}F)_b\\ &=-\{G,F\} \end{aligned}\tag*{$\square$} {F,G}​=ωab(dF)a​(dG)b​=ωba(dF)b​(dG)a​=−ωab(dG)a​(dF)b​=−{G,F}​□

  • {aF1+bF2,G}=a{F1,G}+b{F2,G}\{aF_1+bF_2,G\}=a\{F_1,G\}+b\{F_2,G\}{aF1​+bF2​,G}=a{F1​,G}+b{F2​,G}

    {aF1+bF2,G}=ωab[d(aF1+bF2)]a(dG)b=ωab[d(aF1)]a(dG)b+ωab[d(bF2)]a(dG)b=aωab(dF1)a(dG)b+bωab(dF2)a(dG)b=a{F1,G}+b{F2,G}□\begin{aligned} \{aF_1+bF_2,G\}&=\omega^{ab}[\mathrm{d}(aF_1+bF_2)]_a(\mathrm{d}G)_b\\ &=\omega^{ab}[\mathrm{d}(aF_1)]_a(\mathrm{d}G)_b+\omega^{ab}[\mathrm{d}(bF_2)]_a(\mathrm{d}G)_b\\ &=a\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F_1)_a(\mathrm{d}G)_b+b\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F_2)_a(\mathrm{d}G)_b\\ &=a\{F_1,G\}+b\{F_2,G\} \end{aligned}\tag*{$\square$} {aF1​+bF2​,G}​=ωab[d(aF1​+bF2​)]a​(dG)b​=ωab[d(aF1​)]a​(dG)b​+ωab[d(bF2​)]a​(dG)b​=aωab(dF1​)a​(dG)b​+bωab(dF2​)a​(dG)b​=a{F1​,G}+b{F2​,G}​□

  • {F,GH}={F,G}H+G{F,H}\{F,GH\}=\{F,G\}H+G\{F,H\}{F,GH}={F,G}H+G{F,H}

    {F,GH}=ωab(dF)a[d(GH)]b=ωab(dF)a[HdG+GdH]b=ωab(dF)a(dG)bH+Gωab(dF)a(dH)b={F,G}H+G{F,H}□\begin{aligned} \{F,GH\}&=\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F)_a[\mathrm{d}(GH)]_b\\ &=\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F)_a[H\mathrm{d}G+G\mathrm{d}H]_b\\ &=\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F)_a(\mathrm{d}G)_bH+G\omega^{ab}(\mathrm{d}F)_a(\mathrm{d}H)_b\\ &=\{F,G\}H+G\{F,H\} \end{aligned}\tag*{$\square$} {F,GH}​=ωab(dF)a​[d(GH)]b​=ωab(dF)a​[HdG+GdH]b​=ωab(dF)a​(dG)b​H+Gωab(dF)a​(dH)b​={F,G}H+G{F,H}​□

  • {F,{G,H}}+{G,{H,F}}+{H,{F,G}}=0\{F,\{G,H\}\}+\{G,\{H,F\}\}+\{H,\{F,G\}\}=0{F,{G,H}}+{G,{H,F}}+{H,{F,G}}=0

    由定义

    {F,G}=ωabXGaXFb=(dF)aXGa=XG(F)=−XF(G)\{F,G\}=\omega_{ab}X^a_GX^b_F=(\mathrm{d}F)_aX^a_G=X_G(F)=-X_F(G) {F,G}=ωab​XGa​XFb​=(dF)a​XGa​=XG​(F)=−XF​(G)

    特别地，ωdeXAdXBe={B,A}=−{A,B}\omega_{de}X^d_AX^e_B=\{B,A\}=-\{A,B\}ωde​XAd​XBe​={B,A}=−{A,B}。先计算两个哈密顿向量场的对易子。对定义式 (dG)a=ωabXGb(\mathrm{d}G)_a=\omega_{ab}X^b_G(dG)a​=ωab​XGb​ 两边求李导数

    [LXF(dG)]a=(LXFω)abXGb+ωab[XF,XG]b[\mathcal{L}_{X_F}(\mathrm{d}G)]_a=(\mathcal{L}_{X_F}\omega)_{ab}X^b_G+\omega_{ab}[X_F,X_G]^b [LXF​​(dG)]a​=(LXF​​ω)ab​XGb​+ωab​[XF​,XG​]b

    向量场 XFX_FXF​ 具有保辛性，因此第一项直接消去，故

    ωab[XF,XG]b=[LXF(dG)]a\omega_{ab}[X_F,X_G]^b=[\mathcal{L}_{X_F}(\mathrm{d}G)]_a ωab​[XF​,XG​]b=[LXF​​(dG)]a​

    再用一次嘉当恒等式

    [LXF(dG)]a=XFb[d(dG)]ba+[d(XFb(dG)b)]a=[d(XFb(dG)b)]a=[dXF(G)]a=−(d{F,G})a\begin{aligned} [\mathcal{L}_{X_F}(\mathrm{d}G)]_a &=X^b_F[\mathrm{d}(\mathrm{d}G)]_{ba}+[\mathrm{d}(X^b_F(\mathrm{d}G)_b)]_a\\ &=[\mathrm{d}(X^b_F(\mathrm{d}G)_b)]_a\\ &=[\mathrm{d}X_F(G)]_a\\ &=-(\mathrm{d}\{F,G\})_a \end{aligned} [LXF​​(dG)]a​​=XFb​[d(dG)]ba​+[d(XFb​(dG)b​)]a​=[d(XFb​(dG)b​)]a​=[dXF​(G)]a​=−(d{F,G})a​​

    由于标量场的微分可以唯一指定一个向量场

    ωabX{F,G}b=(d{F,G})a\omega_{ab}X^b_{\{F,G\}}=(\mathrm{d}\{F,G\})_a ωab​X{F,G}b​=(d{F,G})a​

    因此

    ωab[XF,XG]b=−ωabX{F,G}b\omega_{ab}[X_F,X_G]^b=-\omega_{ab}X^b_{\{F,G\}} ωab​[XF​,XG​]b=−ωab​X{F,G}b​

    再由辛形式的非退化性得到

    [XF,XG]a=−X{F,G}a[X_F,X_G]^a=-X^a_{\{F,G\}} [XF​,XG​]a=−X{F,G}a​

    由于辛形式是闭的，(dω)abc=0(\mathrm{d}\omega)_{abc}=0(dω)abc​=0，因此

    (dω)abcXFaXGbXHc=0(\mathrm{d}\omega)_{abc}X^a_FX^b_GX^c_H=0 (dω)abc​XFa​XGb​XHc​=0

    展开左侧

    (dω)abcXFaXGbXHc=XF(ωdeXGdXHe)−XG(ωdeXFdXHe)+XH(ωdeXFdXGe) −ωde[XF,XG]dXHe+ωde[XF,XH]dXGe−ωde[XG,XH]dXFe\begin{aligned} &(\mathrm{d}\omega)_{abc}X^a_FX^b_GX^c_H\\ &=X_F(\omega_{de}X^d_GX^e_H)-X_G(\omega_{de}X^d_FX^e_H)+X_H(\omega_{de}X^d_FX^e_G)\\ &\ -\omega_{de}[X_F,X_G]^dX^e_H+\omega_{de}[X_F,X_H]^dX^e_G-\omega_{de}[X_G,X_H]^dX^e_F \end{aligned} ​(dω)abc​XFa​XGb​XHc​=XF​(ωde​XGd​XHe​)−XG​(ωde​XFd​XHe​)+XH​(ωde​XFd​XGe​) −ωde​[XF​,XG​]dXHe​+ωde​[XF​,XH​]dXGe​−ωde​[XG​,XH​]dXFe​​

    先来看第一行

    XF(ωdeXGdXHe)−XG(ωdeXFdXHe)+XH(ωdeXFdXGe)=XF({H,G})−XG({H,F})+XH({G,F})={{H,G},F}−{{H,F},G}+{{G,F},H}={F,{G,H}}+{G,{H,F}}+{H,{F,G}}\begin{aligned} &X_F(\omega_{de}X^d_GX^e_H)-X_G(\omega_{de}X^d_FX^e_H)+X_H(\omega_{de}X^d_FX^e_G)\\ &=X_F(\{H,G\})-X_G(\{H,F\})+X_H(\{G,F\})\\ &=\{\{H,G\},F\}-\{\{H,F\},G\}+\{\{G,F\},H\}\\ &=\{F,\{G,H\}\}+\{G,\{H,F\}\}+\{H,\{F,G\}\} \end{aligned} ​XF​(ωde​XGd​XHe​)−XG​(ωde​XFd​XHe​)+XH​(ωde​XFd​XGe​)=XF​({H,G})−XG​({H,F})+XH​({G,F})={{H,G},F}−{{H,F},G}+{{G,F},H}={F,{G,H}}+{G,{H,F}}+{H,{F,G}}​

    第二行，利用 [XA,XB]=−X{A,B}[X_A,X_B]=-X_{\{A,B\}}[XA​,XB​]=−X{A,B}​

    −ωde[XF,XG]dXHe+ωde[XF,XH]dXGe−ωde[XG,XH]dXFe=ωdeX{F,G}dXHe−ωdeX{F,H}dXGe+ωdeX{G,H}dXFe=−{{F,G},H}+{{F,H},G}−{{G,H},F}={H,{F,G}}+{G,{H,F}}+{F,{G,H}}\begin{aligned} &-\omega_{de}[X_F,X_G]^dX^e_H+\omega_{de}[X_F,X_H]^dX^e_G-\omega_{de}[X_G,X_H]^dX^e_F\\ &=\omega_{de}X^d_{\{F,G\}}X^e_H-\omega_{de}X^d_{\{F,H\}}X^e_G+\omega_{de}X^d_{\{G,H\}}X^e_F\\ &=-\{\{F,G\},H\}+\{\{F,H\},G\}-\{\{G,H\},F\}\\ &=\{H,\{F,G\}\}+\{G,\{H,F\}\}+\{F,\{G,H\}\} \end{aligned} ​−ωde​[XF​,XG​]dXHe​+ωde​[XF​,XH​]dXGe​−ωde​[XG​,XH​]dXFe​=ωde​X{F,G}d​XHe​−ωde​X{F,H}d​XGe​+ωde​X{G,H}d​XFe​=−{{F,G},H}+{{F,H},G}−{{G,H},F}={H,{F,G}}+{G,{H,F}}+{F,{G,H}}​

    两行结果相同，将它们合并起来

    2({F,{G,H}}+{G,{H,F}}+{H,{F,G}})=02(\{F,\{G,H\}\}+\{G,\{H,F\}\}+\{H,\{F,G\}\})=0 2({F,{G,H}}+{G,{H,F}}+{H,{F,G}})=0

    也就是括号内为零，于是

    {F,{G,H}}+{G,{H,F}}+{H,{F,G}}=0□\begin{aligned} \{F,\{G,H\}\}+\{G,\{H,F\}\}+\{H,\{F,G\}\}=0 \end{aligned}\tag*{$\square$} {F,{G,H}}+{G,{H,F}}+{H,{F,G}}=0​□

至此，我们在相空间上建立了哈密顿力学的基本框架：辛形式把哈密顿量变成哈密顿向量场，它的流保持辛形式和相体积不变；泊松括号则把这一结构表达为标量场之间的代数运算。下一篇，我们将利用这一结构寻找更方便的坐标，讨论正则变换与哈密顿—雅可比方程。
